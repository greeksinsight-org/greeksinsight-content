## How to Subscribe

### Step 1: Navigate to the Pricing Page
***Prequisite: "Make sure you are signed up and logged into Greeksinsight."***

Visit the Greeksinsight website and click on the **"Plans"** icon available on the side bar.

![Figure 5: Plans Icon](/assets/images/plans_icon.png)

### Step 2: Choose Your Plan

Review the features of each plan and select the one that matches your needs:

- Select the **"Billing Cycle"** using the toggle switch on the plan card.
- Click the **"Choose Plan"** button under the Pro/Premium plan.

![Figure 6: Premium Plan Yearly](/assets/images/premium_plan_annual.png)

### Step 3: Enter Payment Information and Complete Your Subscription
Verfiy your plan type, subscription amount and tax, contact and address details.
Securely enter your payment details:

- Credit card information
- Billing address
- Confirm the amount and plan details

![Figure 7: Enter Payment](/assets/images/plan_checkout.png)


### Step 4: Start Using Your Plan

Once subscribed, you can immediately:

1. Log in to your account
2. Access the Greeksinsight dashboard
3. Start using all the features included in your plan
4. Navigate to different analysis tools from the main menu

### Step 5: Switch the Plan

Yes, you can switch between plans:

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to Plans page
3. Choose a plan which is not already selected
4. Confirm the modal for plan switch

***Note: If you get successful message and the plan changes are still not reflected, you can try***
- ***Refresh the website***
- ***Relogin to Greeksinsight***

![Figure 7: Switch Plans](/assets/images/switch_subscription.png)

After successful plan switch you will see a success message below the plan cards

![Figure 8: Switch Plans Success](/assets/images/switch_subscription_success.png)

## Security & privacy notes

- **Payment security:** Your payment information is encrypted and never stored in full by Greeksinsight.
- **Authorized charges:** Only charges authorized by your subscription plan will appear on your statement.
- **Cancellation = no charges:** After cancellation, no further automatic charges will occur after your cancellation effective date.
- **Refund policy:** Refunds follow our refund policy; contact support for details.
- **Tax on purchase:** All the plans offered by Greekisnight are exlusive of taxes, the final amount will be presented on the payments page.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I upgrade my plan?**
Yes! You can upgrade from Pro to Premium anytime after you are logged in to Greeksinsight. Contact our support team for assistance.

**Can I cancel my subscription?**
Yes, you can cancel anytime. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period.

**What payment methods do you accept?**
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, etc.).

**Do you offer a free trial?**
No, as of now we don't offer any free trial.

**What if I need help?**
Our support team is here to help! Visit the Help section or contact us at contact@greeksinsight.com.

## Managing Your Subscription

Once you're subscribed, you can manage your plan from your account settings:

1. Log in to your Greeksinsight account
2. Go to **UserIcon** on bottom left > **Manage Subscription**
3. View your current plan, renewal date, and billing history
4. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription
